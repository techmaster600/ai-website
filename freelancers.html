A note on academic honesty

These tools are meant to help you understand concepts faster, improve your own writing and save time on formatting and research — not to write assignments for you. Submitting AI-generated work as your own can violate your institution's academic integrity policy. When in doubt, check your college's rules before using AI on graded work.
